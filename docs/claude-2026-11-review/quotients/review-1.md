# Review 1 of quotients/plan.md: simplicity and code fit

**Reviewer lens:** Could Q1 be smaller? Is every contract change necessary? Does the plan fit
`intersection.ts`, `pt.ts`, `combiner.ts` and `lighting.ts` as they are written today?
**Read:** the plan, `torus-code/notes.md`, `torus-code/dda_bound_twin.py`, `twin/notes.md` (the
image-set table and §2.5), the skeptic's `quotient-light` section, and the repo files cited
below. Nothing in the repo was edited.

Labels: [checked-code file:line], [checked-num script], [math] (thought through, not checked by
anyone else), [mem].

## Verdict

**Revise before the owner sees it.** The Q1 design fits the code closely, and the light model
(partition MIS, the far clip, the declared truncations) is sound under this lens. These parts
are verified:
- The dispatcher's `hit.t = MAX_DIST` seed and the arms bounded by `hit.t` make a per-cell
  query with a far limit a rename plus one parameter
  [checked-code src/compiler/generate/features/intersection.ts:1680-1688].
- The pt-mis combiner needs no change, because `power_heuristic(a, 0) = 1`. The pt-nee
  combiner needs one condition [checked-code src/components/transport/combiner.ts:33-41].
- Every `lighting_pdf` arm reads the query point only through `q.p` and `light_hit.p`
  [checked-code lighting.ts:647-670].
- NEE builds the image point in x's chart [checked-code techniques/light/light.glsl:20-23].
- The opaque shadow test works with the walk unchanged [checked-code
  shadow/opaque/opaque.glsl:16-19].
- In the media shadow walker, `h.t < remaining` stays right because `h.t` is the total length
  [checked-code shadow/media/media.glsl].
- The pt.ts citations (171, 376) are correct.

The revision is needed for other reasons:
- As written, the static occupant file does not compile in most quotient programs, including
  the plan's own crystal witness and all of Q1b.
- The GLSL sketch is not the walk the twin measured.
- Two pieces add parts that are not needed.
- Three evidence or argument statements are wrong.
- One verification rule would fail on every scene.

None of these changes the design.

## Must fix

**M1. The static `torus.glsl` breaks exact linkage and does not compile in pt and fog programs.**
- The plan puts `scene_intersect`, `scene_intersect_any` and `quotient_reduce` in a static
  occupant file. Static files are included whole (CLAUDE.md, "Hand-written component files are
  included whole").
- The cell form `scene_intersect_any_cell` would exist only where the any-hit query is planned:
  `anyQuery: lighting !== null && !features.media.hasMedia`
  [checked-code src/compiler/plan/Planner.ts:617; intersection.ts:1717].
- So every **pt** quotient program and every **Q1b** program would call an undeclared function,
  which is a GLSL compile error [mem: GLSL ES requires a declaration for every call, even
  inside an unused function]. The pt programs include crystal witness 1 (pt), and the Q1b
  programs all have media.
- In Q1a, `quotient_reduce` is provided with nothing requiring it. That is the same
  unused-seam warning as the open `light_query_medium` defect.
- **Fix:** emit the two walks from the generator, gated exactly as today's
  `scene_intersect_any` is. That was torus-code §5's original placement: "generated by
  intersection.ts, calling the occupant's helpers". Keep only pure math static: the exit
  time, the cell of a point, the image index and shift, and `reduce`. Emit and provide
  `quotient_reduce` only when media exist. A second static file included only under
  `anyQuery` would also work, but it adds a part.

**M2. The GLSL sketch is not the measured walk.**
- `dda_bound_twin.py`, which is the evidence for the loop bound and the float32 precision,
  does two things the sketch drops:
  - It guards zero direction components: `np.where(d != 0, t, big)`.
  - It keeps `t_enter = max(t_enter, t_exit)`.

  [checked-code torus-code/dda_bound_twin.py:21-41]
- The sketch instead relies on "±inf on zero components". That fails when a component is
  **−0.0** [math, IEEE]:
  - `-0.0 >= 0.0` is true, so s = +1.
  - Then `t_next = (positive)/(−0.0) = −inf`, so `t_exit = −inf`.
  - `t_enter` becomes −inf, and the local origin becomes NaN.
- A −0.0 can come out of a mirror reflection off an axis-aligned wall.
- GLSL ES also does not promise IEEE infinities [mem].
- **Fix:** transcribe the twin as written: `d_i != 0.0 ? … : BIG` (the test is true for −0.0
  too) and the monotone `t_enter`. Then state that the bound and the precision numbers apply to
  that form.

**M3. Set `hit.image` once, in the dispatcher and in the walk, not "in every arm".**
- Q1.2 says to "fill `hit.image = 0` in every arm". Q1.1 says "Every arm and the dispatcher
  fill it".
- The image is a fact about the dispatch, not about a shape. `region_from` and `region_to`
  are set by the dispatcher for the same reason [checked-code intersection.ts:1689-1708].
- Filling it in the primitive, mesh, instanced, table and SDF arms adds edits and digest lines
  for nothing, because the walk overwrites the value.
- **Fix:** one line in the non-quotient dispatcher (`hit.image = 0;`) plus the walk's
  assignment. (See S1: Q1 may not need the field at all.)

**M4. The image numbering is written twice.**
- The walk encodes the image as `int(dot(k + 1.0, vec3(9,3,1)))`. NEE and MIS decode it
  through an emitted `QUOTIENT_SHIFT[j]` table, "in the same order" (Q1.3).
- That is two codes that are asserted to agree. It contradicts the plan's own C1 ("the same
  table S, through the same code") and CLAUDE.md's "one answer per fact".
- **Fix:** two adjacent functions in the occupant: `int torus_image_index(vec3 k)` and
  `vec3 torus_image_shift(int j)`, the latter computed as `TORUS_PERIOD * (vec3(j/9,
  (j/3)%3, j%3) - 1.0)`. Remove the emitted table and the descriptor's "27-entry image table":
  data that nothing needs, under the unused-code rule.

**M5. The non-quotient verification rule cannot pass, and two plans move `MAX_DIST`.**
- Moving `#define MAX_DIST 1000.0` out of `src/glsl/core/math.glsl:11` into an emitted define
  moves it to the generated header [checked-code src/compiler/generate/ShaderBuilder.ts:181-197].
  That changes the text of every program. So these two claims are false:
  - Q1.2 / decision 2: "E³ text is unchanged".
  - Q1.5 item 5: "the only differences allowed are `Hit.image` and its fill lines".
- **Fix:** list the moved define as an allowed digest difference. `npm run frames -- compare`
  bit-exact remains the real gate.
- Separately, curved-design step 1 also moves `MAX_DIST`, to the space occupant
  [checked-code docs/claude-2026-11-review/curved-spaces-design.md:140].
  - The plan states the reconciliation (the space supplies the default and `farClip`
    overrides it).
  - It must also say which change lands first. Then one commit moves the define, and the
    other adjusts to it.

**M6. Three evidence or argument statements are wrong.**
1. Q1.4 says the bounds "already exist (… BVH/TLAS roots)".
   - The Validator runs before the App builds any BVH.
   - The scene-data plan hands mesh boxes to the App as `{ geometry: leaf.ref }`
     [checked-code src/compiler/sceneData.ts:140].
   - The Validator has to compute mesh AABBs from `positions` and instance bounds from
     `placements` × the prototype box itself [checked-code src/compiler/types.ts:136-137, 178].
     That is easy, but it is new code. Correct the citation and the line estimate.
2. "What is cut" says in-cell-only NEE "is worse than pt per work in E³ (NEAR 1.14)". NEAR is
   the *nearest* image, not the in-cell image. S1 was measured only in SW
   [checked-code twin/notes.md:101-103, 227, 270]. Relabel the row: E³ in-cell was not
   measured.
3. Q1.1 says "Objects keep a margin from ∂D, so every surface vertex is strictly inside D".
   - This is false once decision 4's spanning walls exist: a hit on a wall can lie on a face
     of D.
   - The conclusion still holds, for a different reason [math]. The walk's `k` is an absolute
     cell index in the coordinates of the ray. Those are x's own coordinates, because the BSDF
     ray is spawned from `hit.p` and `prev_query.p = hit.p`. So `k` is the image relative to
     x even when x or the spawned origin lies a rounding error outside D.
   - Restate the argument this way. That also covers Q1b's reduced medium points, which have
     no margin from the faces.
   - (Minor, not a must-fix: where a wall crosses a face, a hit rounded just past `t_exit`
     reappears at local t ≈ 0 in the next cell. An arm with a positive tmin would miss it.
     This affects a band of about 1e-7·L [math]. State it or ignore it.)

## Should consider (simplicity)

**S1. Q1 needs no change to the locked `Hit` type.** [thought through, not checked]
- In E³, `lighting_pdf(q, wi, light_id, light_hit)` already has everything it needs to compute
  the image itself [checked-code lighting.ts:647 signature]:
  `k = round((q.p + wi * light_hit.t − light_hit.p) / TORUS_PERIOD)`.
  - `q.p` is the stored previous vertex.
  - `light_hit.t` is the total length from the spawned origin.
  - The error is at most the spawn offset plus farClip·ε, which is far below L/2. So the
    rounding is exact.
- The pt-nee line calls the same function with `s.prev_query.p`, `s.ray.direction` and `hit`.
- What this saves:
  - No `structs.glsl` edit, and no dispatcher fill line.
  - The non-quotient digest differs only by the moved `MAX_DIST`.
  - The locked-type decision moves to Q2, where it is really needed. Q2 can then add `image`
    and `arrival` in one change instead of two.
- The plan's objection is that this "derives a second time what the walk knows". But the walk
  does not need the image; it is computed only here, so this would be the one place. The
  owner should see the two options with equal weight.

**S2. The crystal witness is the heaviest part of Q1's verification, and something lighter
gives the same exactness.**
- Witness 1 needs three things:
  - `farClip` honored in non-quotient programs (decision 2 cites the witness as the reason).
  - A generator for roughly 21,000 instances.
  - A calibrated rmse threshold.
- The skeptic proposed the crystal for *finite-horizon* scenes. Take three orthogonal spanning
  walls whose room straddles D's faces. The crystal of that scene is a single closed box room
  in plain E³ [math]:
  - It is exact under identical streams.
  - It needs no `farClip` outside quotients and no instancing.
  - It still tests the pairings, because the room's contents are seen across faces.
- This is torus-code §7 witness 4, which the plan dropped. Add torus-code witness 1 (a
  first-hit window or lattice-visibility mean against a float64 TS twin; Q2.6 uses the same
  pattern) to cover long segments.
- Drawback: it depends on decision 4, and it does not test long multi-bounce paths across
  many cells the way the open crystal does. [thought through, not checked]

**S3. Decide whether a new component family is worth it for one occupant.**
- A new family `src/components/quotient/` brings a README contract, a registry and an entry
  in `tests/components/structure.test.ts` FAMILIES.
- The quotient is a property of the space (`ambientSpace.quotient`), and
  `src/components/ambient/` already exists, with only `euclidean/` in it.
- Ask the owner. Either answer is defensible.

**S4. Q2: say which readers `Hit.arrival` replaces.** Curved step 1 respells `wo =
-s.ray.direction` and four other readers as `ambient_flow(ray, t).direction`
[checked-code curved-spaces-design.md:134-136]. Q2 should state that `hit.arrival` replaces
only the `wo` site, and say why the others are safe:
- The dispatcher's side test sees the local ray.
- The null re-spawn and the media walker are compile errors in Q2.

Otherwise there are two answers for "arrival direction".

**S5. Small items.**
- The rename to `…_cover` touches the power/uniform sampler (lighting.ts:552-557), the
  tree's sampler (887) and both pdf generators (647, 911). Skies are errors in quotients, so
  the `selectionLive` wrappers never occur there. The +40 lines is plausible.
- The "equiangular" row is mostly redundant: the existing pin already rejects equiangular
  with samplable area emitters [checked-code Validator.ts:507-529], and quotients forbid delta
  lights.
- Reusing the remainder of `xi.x` is unbiased, but it costs about 5 bits of the light tree's
  descent random number. A separate `random()` is just as simple. Optional.

## Checked and fine under this lens

- Renaming the dispatcher only in quotient programs keeps non-quotient text unchanged (apart
  from M5).
- `scene_region_at` probes stay in the local chart under the margin rule.
- Camera rays are not read by MIS: `prev_was_delta` [checked-code combiner.ts:34].
- Light-tree queries take the moved `LightQuery` [checked-code lighting.ts:786, 804].
- In Q1b, the media shadow walker calls the full `scene_intersect`. In a quotient that could
  walk to `farClip`, but the shadow ray is aimed at the light, so it stops at the light just
  past `remaining`. No cost problem [math].
- Q1b's two `quotient_reduce` call sites (pt.ts:171, 376) are the only pt.ts edits, and they
  fit.
